# Diagnose

Purpose: map where visibility is actually breaking down before proposing anything.

## Inputs

- Current positioning (or lack of it)
- Existing content library
- Current distribution channels
- Operational reality (booking, CRM, sales process, team)
- Competitive landscape

## Outputs

- **Visibility breakdown map** — where the system leaks
- Prioritized fixes — what actually moves the needle
- Scope recommendation — Diagnose / Build / Compound fit

## What we're looking for

1. **Positioning leaks** — unclear who the business is for, why it matters, or why a competitor can't say the same thing
2. **Content leaks** — content exists but isn't built to be found or redistributed
3. **Distribution leaks** — great work never reaches the right people
4. **Operational friction** — visibility isn't wired into how the business runs (booking, response, follow-up, CRM)

## Non-negotiable

- A map, not a deck
- Evidence, not opinions
- We tell you where visibility is breaking — even if it's uncomfortable
