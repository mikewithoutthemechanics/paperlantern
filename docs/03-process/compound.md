# Compound

Purpose: stay in the system as it runs, refining instead of resetting every quarter.

## What we do monthly

1. **Operate** — run the exposure system (content, distribution, amplification)
2. **Measure** — track outcomes, not vanity metrics
3. **Refine** — adjust positioning, content, and distribution based on real data
4. **Scale** — use AI amplification to increase output without scaling headcount
5. **Report** — show the work, show the numbers

## Why compound instead of reset

Most agencies reset every quarter: new campaign, new calendar, new deck.
Compounding means each month builds on the last — visibility, trust, and inbound get stronger over time.

## Client responsibilities

- Access to the operational systems (CRM, analytics, booking, accounts)
- Timely feedback on strategic direction
- A "we ship things" culture — not a committee that approves decks

## Definition of done (per month)

- System ran
- Evidence captured
- Real improvements made
- Client sees outcomes, not just activities
