# Case Study — Punctual Plumbers

<punctual-plumbers.co.za>

## Situation

A local plumbing business was invisible online: no differentiated position, no system for converting inbound, no distribution wired into operations.

## Diagnosis

- Positioned as just another "local plumber" — nothing a competitor couldn't copy
- No booking system or conversion infrastructure
- Visibility wasn't connected to operations — slow response, manual follow-up, lost jobs

## Build

- Repositioned the brand as the only trades brand in the region running a real booking system
- Shipped the website, job management system, and booking flow
- Built infrastructure, not a brochure page

## Distribution

- Wired local SEO and answer-engine visibility directly into the booking flow
- Made visibility part of how the business operates, not a separate marketing activity

## Results

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Conversion | +22% |
| First response | 95% faster |
| Jobs booked | 290+ |
