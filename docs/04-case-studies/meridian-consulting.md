## Situation

A B2B consulting firm with strong delivery talent but weak visibility. Inbound was inconsistent, competitors owned the "reliable expert" positioning, and content existed but wasn't distributed or wired into sales.

## Diagnosis

- Positioned as "another consultancy" — nothing a competitor couldn't say
- Existing content was publish-and-forget, not built to be found or redistributed
- No system connecting content → leads → booked calls
- Distribution was scattered across channels with no operational wiring

## Build

- Repositioned the firm around a specific, defensible niche
- Rebuilt the content engine — built to be found and redistributed
- Wired distribution into LinkedIn and answer-engine presence
- Shipped the infrastructure connecting content → lead capture → booking flow

## Distribution

- LinkedIn founder-led and firm-led presence
- Answer-engine visibility for the niche questions clients actually ask
- Distribution wired into the sales pipeline, not bolted on
