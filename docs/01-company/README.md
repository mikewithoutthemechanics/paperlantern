## One-liner

PaperLantern builds the positioning, content, and distribution infrastructure most agencies only strategize about.

## What we do

We don't hand you a deck. We ship the system — and show our work.

An exposure system is the infrastructure that makes visibility compound instead of evaporate:

- **Positioning** that's actually differentiated
- **Content** built to be redistributed
- **Distribution** wired into how you already operate

We build it once. It keeps working.

## What we don't do

- One-off campaigns
- Vanity metrics
- Decks as deliverables
- Content calendars that reset every quarter
