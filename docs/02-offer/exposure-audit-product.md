A low-barrier, paid first product that feeds the main Build/Compound offer.

- **Is** a foot in the door for Build/Compound when the fit is right

## What's included

1. **Mini visibility audit** — positioning, content, distribution, operations
2. **10–15 page visibility breakdown** — where you're invisible, what's losing you jobs
3. **Priority fixes list** — what to do first, what can wait
4. **30–45 min walkthrough call** — we go through it together

## Delivery

- **Turnaround:** 5 business days from payment + completed intake form
- **Format:** PDF or Google Doc + walkthrough call
- **After the audit:** clear, honest recommendation — Build/Compound if it's a fit, DIY roadmap if not
