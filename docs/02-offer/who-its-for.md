# Who It's For

Not every business needs an exposure system. Some businesses already have one. Here's who gets the most out of working with PaperLantern.

## Ideal clients

- **Service businesses that live or die on inbound** — trades, consultancies, agencies, law firms, clinics, and similar
- **Founders doing their own marketing** — people who want to stop starting from scratch every quarter
- **Companies with existing content but no distribution** — the hardest gap to close
- **Companies ready to ship, not just think** — they want the machine, not the deck

## Signature in

- Regional or niche market leaders (or would-be leaders)
- Operators who value outcomes over vanity metrics
- Businesses that trust process over luck

## Not for

- One-off campaign buyers
- Teams shopping for a content calendar
- Companies that want a recommendations document and nothing else
- Anyone looking for a "rebrand and leave" studio
