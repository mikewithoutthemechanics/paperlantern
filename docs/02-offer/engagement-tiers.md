# Engagement Tiers

PaperLantern works in three phases. Every engagement is a system, not a one-off.

| | **Diagnose** | **Build** | **Compound** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **What** | Exposure audit / visibility breakdown map | Ships the system | Runs it, refines it |
| **Duration** | ~1–2 weeks | ~4–8 weeks | Ongoing / retainer |
| **Deliverable** | A map, not a deck | Working infrastructure | Compound growth, refined monthly |

## Tier 01 — Diagnose

**Purpose:** Map where visibility is actually breaking down before proposing anything.

**What we do:**
- Interview the business and its operations
- Audit current positioning, content, and distribution
- Identify the visibility bottlenecks — not the symptoms
- Produce a **visibility breakdown map** (not a speculative deck)

**Client outcome:** A clear picture of where visibility breaks and where investment will actually move the needle.

---

## Tier 02 — Build

**Purpose:** Design and ship the exposure system itself.

**What we do:**
- Lock in differentiated positioning
- Design content to be found and redistributed
- Wire distribution into how the client already operates
- Build supporting infrastructure (site, booking, CRM, tracking, publishing)

**Client outcome:** Working infrastructure that generates visibility — not a recommendations document.

---

## Tier 03 — Compound

**Purpose:** Stay in the system as it runs, refining instead of resetting.

**What we do:**
- Operate the system month-to-month
- Refine positioning, content, and distribution based on real data
- Scale output through AI amplification without scaling headcount
- Report on outcomes, not vanity metrics

**Client outcome:** Visibility that compounds instead of evaporating.
