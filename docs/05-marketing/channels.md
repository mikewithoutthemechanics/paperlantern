# Channels

How PaperLantern reaches **service businesses** across South Africa, UAE, UK, and Australia.

## Positioning for service businesses

Service businesses don't need "more marketing." They need to be **found, trusted, and converted** at the exact moment a customer is looking. PaperLantern's channels are built around that reality.

---

## Primary channels

### 1. Website (paperlantern.xyz)
- The home base for the offer
- Case studies front and center — show the work
- Clear Diagnose → Build → Compound offering
- Currency-aware pricing (USD base, ZAR for South African visitors)

### 2. LinkedIn (firm + founder-led)
- Best for reaching owners/decision-makers of service businesses
- Michael Kidd as founder voice
- Posts: exposure breakdowns, before/after logs, "how visibility compounds"
- Target: trades, agencies, consultancies, law firms, clinics, regional brands

### 3. Google Business Profile + Local SEO
- For regional reach in South Africa, UAE, UK, and Australia
- Presence in locations where service businesses search locally
- Proof: Punctual Plumbers case — local visibility wired into booking

### 4. Answer-engine visibility (Google, ChatGPT/Perplexity etc.)
- Service-business owners increasingly ask "what is X", "who does X in my area"
- Content built to be found — not just posted
- PaperLantern applies this to itself before selling it

---

## Content engine

- **The Exposure System** — what it is, why tactics fail, why it compounds
- **Show the Work** — case studies, logs, before/after numbers
- **Service-business plays** — local plumbers, agencies, consultants, clinics
- **Founder voice** — Michael Kidd on engineering visibility, not guessing at it

---

## Outreach (initial)

- **Direct outreach** to service businesses already spending on tactics (ads, SEO retainer, content) but lacking a system
- **Referral** from the Punctual Plumbers result and any early wins
- **In-market proof** — publish case studies before pitching, so cold conversations arrive with evidence
