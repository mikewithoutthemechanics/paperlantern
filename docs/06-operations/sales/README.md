# Sales & Pipeline

How PaperLantern qualifies, prices, and closes work.

## Pipeline stages

1. **Outbound** — cold/warm outreach sent
2. **Interest** — prospect replies or refers
3. **Chat** — 10-minute qualifier call
4. **Exposure Audit booked** — paid Diagnose engagement
5. **Proposal** — Build/Compound scoped and priced
6. **Signed** — agreement in place
7. **Onboarding** — kickoff begins

---

## Stage details

### 1. Outbound

- Use `docs/05-marketing/outreach-emails.md` templates (cold + warm + follow-up)
- Target: service businesses (trades, consultancies, agencies, law firms, clinics, regional brands)
- Track: who's been contacted, replied, followed up

**Goal:** get 10-minute chats, not immediate sales.

### 2. Interest

Someone responds or is referred. Log it immediately.

**Next action:** book the 10-minute chat.

### 3. Chat (10-minute qualifier)

**Purpose:** qualify fit, surface the pain, invite to Diagnose.

**Must learn:**
- What does the business do?
- Is visibility actually a problem (losing jobs to competitors)?
- Do they already spend on tactics (ads, SEO, content)?
- Do they have operational infrastructure (booking, CRM, response flow)?
- Are they ready to build a system, or just shopping for a tactic?
- Fit for our markets (South Africa, UAE, UK, Australia)?

**Qualified if:**
- Service business ✅
- Visibility pain is real and current ✅
- Decision-maker is present ✅
- Open to a system, not just a tactic ✅

**Next step:** offer the Exposure Audit (paid Diagnose).

## Checklists

- [ ] Lead logged
- [ ] Outreach sent / response handled
- [ ] 10-min chat booked or declined
- [ ] Fit verified (service business, real pain, decision-maker ready)
- [ ] Exposure Audit proposed and priced
- [ ] Audit delivered (map, not deck)
- [ ] Build/Compound proposal sent

- [ ] Onboarding scheduled
