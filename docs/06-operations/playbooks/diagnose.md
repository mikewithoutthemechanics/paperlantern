# Diagnose Playbook

Purpose: run the Exposure Audit from start to finish.

**Deliverable:** a visibility breakdown map — a map, not a deck.

---

## Step 1 — Kickoff call

- Meet the key people
- Confirm goals and context
- Set expectations: output is a map, not a deck
- Collect access to existing assets (site, analytics, CRM, booking, socials)

## Step 2 — Business context

- What does the business sell?
- Who are the customers?
- Who are the main competitors?
- Why do customers choose them (or not)?
- What does "good" look like in 6–12 months?

## Step 3 — Audit: Positioning

- Can they describe who they are in one sentence a competitor can't copy?
- What's the current positioning story?
- Is it defensible? Differentiated? Said anywhere consistently?

## Step 4 — Audit: Content

- What content exists?
- Is it built to be found (search/answer engines)?
- Is it built to be redistributed?
- Is it publish-and-forget or part of a system?

## Step 5 — Audit: Distribution

- Where do customers actually look?
- What channels does the business use?
- Which channels are wired into operations (booking, CRM, response)?
- What's the gap between where customers are and where the business is visible?

## Step 6 — Audit: Operations

- How fast do they respond to inquiries?
- Do they have a real booking flow or manual chaos?
- Is CRM/tracking in place?
- Does visibility connect to revenue, or just "brand"?

## Step 7 — Build the map

Identify where visibility breaks. Prioritize by impact:

1. **Critical** — system is leaking the most revenue
2. **Important** — needs fixing to sustain growth
3. **Nice-to-have** — can wait

## Step 8 — Present

- Show the map: where visibility breaks
- Show the priority fixes
- Recommend Diagnose / Build / Compound fit
- If fit is clear, propose next step

---

## Deliverable checklist

- [ ] Visibility breakdown map delivered (document, not a deck)
- [ ] Priorities ranked by impact
- [ ] Recommendation for Build/Compound clear
- [ ] Case-study-worthy facts logged (for future proof, with client permission)
