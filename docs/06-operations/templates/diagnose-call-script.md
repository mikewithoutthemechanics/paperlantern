# Diagnose Call Script

Two scripts in one: the **10-minute qualifier** and the **paid Exposure Audit call**.

---

## Part A — 10-minute qualifier (free)

**Goal:** qualify fit, surface pain, offer the paid audit.

### Open

> "Thanks for the time. I'll keep this to 10 minutes. I want to understand how [business] gets customers today — then I can tell you honestly if an exposure audit makes sense."

### Questions (pick, don't power-walk through all)

1. "What does [business] do, and who do you serve?"
2. "How do you get new customers today?" (listen for: referrals only, no system, no online presence)
3. "See competitors winning jobs online that should be yours?" (confirm pain)
4. "What have you tried in the last 12 months? What happened?"
5. "Do you have a way to track enquiries and responses?" (listen for chaos → confirms problem)

### Qualify check (mental)

- [ ] Service business?
- [ ] Real, current visibility pain?
- [ ] Decision-maker on the call?
- [ ] Open to a system, not just a tactic?

### Close to audit (if qualified)

> "This sounds like a good fit. The first step is an Exposure Audit — we map where visibility is breaking and priority fixes. It's [price], takes about [X days], and you get a map, not a deck. Want me to book it?"

### Close to nurture (if not ready)

> "No problem. I'll send you a short explainer so you know what an exposure system is. If it ever makes sense, you know where to find me."

(→ move to nurture sequence, not dead-email pile)

---

## Part B — Paid Exposure Audit call

**Goal:** gather the info to build the visibility breakdown map.

### Warm open

> "Let's start where you are. I'll ask questions, you answer honestly — the map is only as good as the input."

### Topics to cover (from intake + onboarding survey)

1. **Business reality** — what they do, customers, differentiation
2. **The pain** — why they think visibility is broken, who's winning
3. **Website/enquiry flow** — do people find them? Can visitors convert?
4. **Operations** — response time, booking, CRM, follow-up
5. **Content** — what exists, where it lives
6. **Distribution** — where customers look, where the business is present
7. **Competitors** — who wins, why

### Close

> "I'll turn this into the visibility breakdown map. You'll get: where you're breaking, priority fixes, and what I'd recommend. That's it."

---

## Rules

- Listen more than talk. It's an audit call, not a showcase.
- Ask follow-ups when something smells like the real bottleneck.
- Don't promise anything you can't ship.
- If it's not a fit, say so honestly.
