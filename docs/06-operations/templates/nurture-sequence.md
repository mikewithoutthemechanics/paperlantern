# Email Nurture Sequence — Template

For leads who don't book right away. Keeps you top-of-mind without being spammy.

**Trigger:** no reply after the outreach follow-up sequence, or lead marked "Nurture."

---

## Sequence: 6 touches over ~3 months

| Touch | Day | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 | Small useful insight — no pitch |
| 2 | Day 14 | Short case-study highlight (Punctual Plumbers) |
| 3 | Day 30 | "What's an exposure system?" one-pager |
| 4 | Day 45 | Personal note — "saw [specific thing about them]" |
| 5 | Day 60 | Another result post / relevant insight |
| 6 | Day 75 | Low-pressure re-offer: "still open to a 10-min chat?" |

Then: move to cold / stop if no engagement after re-offer.

---

## Rules

- Value first: most touches should NOT be "book a call"
- Personalize where possible — no blast feeling
- Unsubscribe / stop every time if they ask
- If they reply, move out of nurture immediately (they're warm now)

---

## Nurture content ideas

- Exposure breakdowns (short, real examples)
- Proof screenshots / numbers (client-approved)
- "What's an exposure system?" explainer
- The no-deck philosophy
- Founder voice: what Michael's seeing in the market
