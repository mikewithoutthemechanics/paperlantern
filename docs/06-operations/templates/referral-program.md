# Referral Program — Ask Template

Simple, low-friction way to ask happy clients for referrals.

---

## When to ask

- After a win (job booked, target hit, report delivered with good numbers)
- In the offboarding / lifecycle survey
- On a case-study permission request

---

## The ask (email/message copy)

> Hi [Name],
>
> We hit [specific win] together — that's exactly the kind of result that happens when the system runs.
>
> Do you know anyone else who's invisible online and losing jobs to competitors? I'd be happy to give them a quick Exposure Audit.
>
> No pressure, but if they book through you, we'll [incentive — discount, credit, referral fee, or just a coffee].
>
> — Michael

---

## Incentive options

- [ ] No-cost mention (client's name on our site / case study)
- [ ] Referral credit: [e.g., 10% off next month]
- [ ] Referral fee: [e.g., 10% of first invoice]
- [ ] Just a thank-you / coffee

*Keep it simple. Most referrals come from a good relationship, not a complex program.*

---

## Referral flow

1. Client refers someone
2. New lead enters EspoCRM marked "referral from [name]"
3. Outreach template (warm) is used
4. If booked → thank the referrer + apply incentive
5. Log referrer for future relationship / rewards

---

## Rules

- Never spam referrals. One ask at the right moment beats ten generic asks.
- Always thank the referrer, even if the lead doesn't close.
- Keep incentives simple enough to actually pay out.
