# Client Kickoff Agenda — [Client]

The first call with a new client. Keeps it structured and skips the awkward discovery of "what do you do?"

**Duration:** 30–45 min
**Attendees:** [Client contact + decision-maker], Michael Kidd

---

## Agenda

### 1. Quick intro + context (5 min)

- Confirm what they do and who their customers are
- Confirm the problem that brought them here

### 2. What we're shipping (5 min)

- Review the engagement (Diagnose / Build / Compound)
- Confirm the deliverable is a map/system — not a deck

### 3. Access & tooling (5 min)

- What access do we need? (website, analytics, CRM, booking, socials)
- What tools do they use today?
- Set up shared workspace / folder

### 4. How we work (5 min)

- Weekly check-in rhythm
- Monthly reporting
- Communication standards (1 business day, no surprises)

### 5. What we need from them (5 min)

- Feedback timeframes
- Decision-maker availability
- "Ship things" culture — not a committee

### 6. Definition of success (5 min)

- What does "good" look like? (their words)
- Which metric would they most want to move?

### 7. Next steps (5 min)

- First deliverable
- Next check-in
- Open questions / blockers
