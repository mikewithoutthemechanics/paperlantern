# Client Exit Survey + Testimonial Request

Used at two moments:
1. **Offboarding** — when an engagement ends
2. **Milestone proof** — when there's a meaningful win worth capturing

Keep it short. A client who has to write a paragraph is unlikely to write a good quote.

---

## Part A — Exit survey (offboarding only)

1. On a scale of 1–10, how likely are you to recommend PaperLantern?
2. What was the biggest win from working together?
3. What could we have done better?
4. Would you work with us again? Why / why not?
5. Anything else?

---

## Part B — Testimonial / proof request (when relevant)

> "We build systems that produce outcomes. Would you be open to saying a sentence or two about the results you saw? We only use it if you're comfortable."

**Example prompts:**
- "The biggest change was _"
- "Before PaperLantern, we _"
- "What surprised you most?"

**Preferred format:**
- 1–3 sentences
- Specific numbers if possible ("we booked 290+ jobs", "response time dropped 95%")
- Permission to use: [ ] website, [ ] LinkedIn, [ ] case study, [ ] sales outreach

---

## Logging

- Save answers to `Nextcloud / Proof Log /`
- If permission granted, add to case-study template and marketing folder
- If anything falls outside expectations, flag for internal review

---

## Rules

- Never publish a client quote without explicit permission
- Ask at the right moment — right after a win, not months later
- Keep it low-friction: make it as easy as possible for the client to say yes
