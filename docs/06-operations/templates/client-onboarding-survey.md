# Client Onboarding Survey

Sent to a new client before kickoff. Gathers operating context that speeds up the Build.

**Time to complete:** ~10 minutes.

---

## Business reality

1. What does your business actually do, day to day? (short, practical answer)
2. Who is your ideal customer? (describe them)
3. What makes you different from competitors? (your words, not polished)
4. What do customers say they like most about you?

## Operations

5. How do enquiries currently come in? (phone, WhatsApp, email, form, other)
6. How fast do you usually respond?
7. Who handles bookings / sales / follow-up?
8. What tools do you use today? (website, booking, CRM, accounting, calendar)

## Content & channels

9. What content do you have already? (website copy, blog, socials, flyers, anything)
10. Where do your customers typically look when they need you?
11. Have you tried any marketing in the last 2 years? What happened?

## Goals

12. If this system works, what changes in your business? (6 months)
13. What's the one metric you'd most want to move?
14. Any constraints we should know about? (budget, timeline, team, approval process)

---

## What happens next

We use this to speed up Diagnosis and Build. It's not a sales call — it's operating context.
