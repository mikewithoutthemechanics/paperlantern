**Between PaperLantern and [Client Name]**

## 1. Parties

- **PaperLantern** (Michael Kidd, Founder), based in South Africa
- **[Client Name]**, based in [Country]

## 2. Purpose

The parties intend to discuss a potential or ongoing engagement regarding exposure systems, positioning, content, distribution, and AI amplification.

## 3. Confidential information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public business information shared between the parties, including but not limited to:

- Business plans and financial data
- Customer and client lists
- Product/service details
- Pricing and terms
- Systems, workflows, and operational data
- Any information marked confidential or reasonably understood to be confidential

## 4. Obligations

Each party agrees to:

1. Use Confidential Information only for the purpose above
2. Not disclose it to third parties except as needed for the engagement or as required by law
3. Protect it with the same care used for its own confidential information
4. Return or destroy it upon written request (subject to legal/backup retention requirements)

## 5. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

- Is or becomes public through no breach
- Was already known without obligation
- Was independently developed without use of Confidential Information
- Is required to be disclosed by law

## 6. Term

This agreement applies during the engagement and for **[X]** years after, unless the information is a trade secret (then for as long as it remains a trade secret).

## 7. No license

Nothing in this agreement grants a license to any intellectual property.

## 8. Miscellaneous

- Governing law: [Jurisdiction / South Africa]
- Entire agreement: this NDA supersedes prior oral/written discussions on confidentiality
- Survival: obligations survive termination of any engagement
