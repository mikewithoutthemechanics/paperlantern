# Goals & Metrics Tracker — PaperLantern

Internal tracking: how the business itself is performing. Not client-facing.

---

## North-star metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue per month | Gross income after refunds/fees | [per month] |
| Active clients | Paying engagements (Compound + Build in progress) | [count] |
| Pipeline value | Open opportunities in EspoCRM (expected value) | [amount] |
| Net revenue per client | Revenue − direct delivery costs | [amount] |

---

## Pipeline metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Leads / month | New qualified leads entering pipeline | [count] |
| Show rate | 10-min chats / leads | [%] |
| Audit conversion | Exposure Audits sold / chats | [%] |
| Audit→Build | Builds sold / audits delivered | [%] |
| Time-to-close | Lead → signed (days) | [days] |

## Delivery quality

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| On-time delivery | Deliverables shipped by deadline | [%] |
| Response time | Client messages answered within 1 business day | [%] |
| Client satisfaction | Lifecycle survey avg / testimonial quality | [score] |

## Cash & ops

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| ARR / MRR | Annualized / monthly recurring | [amount] |
| Invoice aging | Days overdue, total value | [track] |
| Utilization | Client work hours / total work hours | [%] |
| Backup reliability | Restore test passes | [100%] |

---

## Review cadence

- **Monthly** — any metric green/red, top decisions for next month
- **Quarterly** — strategy: pricing, markets, offers, stack
- **Yearly** — healthy business check: margins, positioning, direction

---

## Rules

- Few metrics, well-chosen — don't dashboard everything
- Outcome-focused, not activity-focused
- If a metric isn't moving a decision, stop tracking it
