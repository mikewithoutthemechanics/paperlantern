## What's an exposure system?

Most agencies sell tactics — a campaign, a rebrand, a content calendar that resets every quarter.

An exposure system is the infrastructure that makes visibility compound instead of evaporate:
- **Positioning** that's actually differentiated
- **Content** built to be redistributed
- **Distribution** wired into how you already operate

We build it once. It keeps working.

## How is this different from a marketing agency?

Agencies hand you a deck. We ship the system — and show our work.

We're not selling campaigns or tactics. We're building the infrastructure that makes visibility compound over time.
