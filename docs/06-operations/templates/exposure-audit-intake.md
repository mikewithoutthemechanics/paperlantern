# Exposure Audit — Intake Questionnaire

Sent to prospective clients before a Diagnose call. Collects the context needed to make the audit call productive.

**Do not** over-collect. Answering this should take 10–15 minutes.

---

## About your business

1. What does your business do, in one sentence?
2. What do you sell, and to whom?
3. Where are you located / where do you serve?
4. How long have you been operating?

## The problem

5. What makes you think your visibility is broken?
6. How do you currently get new customers? (referrals, Google, ads, word of mouth, other)
7. Who are your main competitors?
8. Why do customers choose them over you?

## Current setup

9. Do you have a website? If yes, share the URL.
10. Do you have a booking system / enquiry flow? If yes, describe briefly.
11. Do you use a CRM or track enquiries anywhere?
12. What marketing have you tried in the last 12 months? (what worked, what didn't?)

## Goals

13. What does "good" look like in 6–12 months? (jobs, revenue, leads, something else)
14. What would you want to happen before spending money on more marketing?
15. Anything else we should know?

---

## What happens next

We'll review this before our call, then walk through where visibility is breaking during the **Exposure Audit** — and what it would take to fix it.

This is not a pitch. It's a map.
