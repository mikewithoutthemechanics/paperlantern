# AI Amplification SOP

How PaperLantern uses AI systems to scale positioning, content, distribution, and operations.

**Principle:** AI scales output without scaling headcount. It never replaces judgment or the human proof.

---

## Where AI is used

| Area | What AI does | What humans still own |
|---|---|---|
| Positioning | Drafting messaging variants, stress-testing differentiation | Final positioning decisions |
| Content | Drafting, repurposing, outlining, SEO/answer-engine research | Facts, voice, judgment, final edits |
| Distribution | Channel-specific repurposing, scheduling drafts, personalization | Actual sending, relationships |
| Operations | Drafting reports, summarizing calls, tracking follow-ups | Interpretation, client judgment, final approval |

---

## AI amplification loop (per engagement)

1. **Input** — gather raw material: client facts, call notes, data, existing content
2. **Generate** — AI drafts variations and repurposes across channels
3. **Judge** — human (PaperLantern or client) selects what's true, on-brand, useful
4. **Ship** — approved output goes live
5. **Log** — what worked goes into playbooks and case-study evidence

---

## Rules

- **Never publish AI output without human review.** Ever.
- **Facts come from humans or verified data.** AI doesn't invent numbers.
- **Voice is PaperLantern's.** AI assists, Michael / team owns the voice.
- **Show the work.** If AI scaled content 5x, we can say so — with evidence.
- **Client data stays inside approved tools.** No sharing client data with unapproved AI tools.
