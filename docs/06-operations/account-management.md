# Account Management & Client Communication Standards

How PaperLantern communicates with clients across the engagement.

---

## Response-time standards

| Touchpoint | Standard |
|---|---|
| Client email / message | Reply within 1 business day |
| Urgent issue | Same day, acknowledge immediately |
| Weekly check-in invite / agenda | Sent at least 24h before |
| Monthly report | Delivered monthly, same week each month |

---

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| Monday | Internal priorities set for client work |
| Mid-week | Client work executes (run the system) |
| Thursday/Friday | Client check-in or weekly summary (if in Compound) |

During **Build**, use a structured weekly check-in:
- What shipped this week
- What's next
- Blockers / decisions needed from client

---

## Client communication rules

- **Small words, big clarity.** No jargon unless we explain it once and use it consistently.
- **Show the work.** Numbers and evidence beat adjectives.
- **Bad news first.** If something slipped or underperformed, lead with it — then the fix.
- **No surprises.** If a deadline moves or scope grows, say so in writing immediately.
- **Client sees the map, not just the destination.** We show the system working, not just the result.

---

## What we never promise

- Guaranteed rank #1
- "Viral" growth
- Results in a week
- Any outcome we can't measure

---

## Client responsibilities (recap)

- Access to systems (CRM, analytics, booking, accounts)
- Feedback within agreed timeframes
- A "ship things" culture, not a committee that approves decks

---

## Escalation path

If a client is unhappy or a deliverable is at risk:
1. Account lead acknowledges within 1 business day
2. Root cause identified within 2 business days
3. Written plan + timeline shared
4. Founder (Michael Kidd) involved if unresolved
