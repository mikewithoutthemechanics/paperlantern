# First 3 Exposure Audit Customers — Acquisition Plan

Goal: **3 paid Exposure Audits ($495 each) in the first 30 days.**

Why 3: enough to validate the offer, produce 2–3 case studies, and fund momentum. Prove → publish → repeat.

---

## Strategy in one line

Pick the most visible, most desperate service business in one market, make them feel genuinely seen, and sell them a $495 map before they spend on another tactic.

---

## Target selection

### Ideal profile

- **Service business** (trades, consultancy, clinic, law, agency)
- In **South Africa first** (home market, easiest wins, local proof already exists)
- **Losing work to competitors** — or at least suspects it
- **Has a website** but no booking/CRM/system
- **Operator-led** (owner makes decisions, no committee)
- Within **reach of 5 days of work** (audit deliverable)

### How to find them

1. **Google Maps** — search "plumber [city]", "consultant [city]", "clinic [city]"
2. **Sort by reviews** but with weak websites / no booking (visible gap)
3. **Local Facebook groups** — owners posting "who's good at X?" → find the businesses people complain about or are underserved
4. **LinkedIn** — owner-run service firms with weak content presence
5. **Existing referral** — ask anyone who knows a service-business owner

## Outreach sequence (per candidate)

### Day 0 — Personalized cold/warm email

See `docs/05-marketing/outreach-emails.md` templates. Use a **specific observation**:

> "I noticed [COMPANY] has [specific gap — e.g., no booking on your site], while [COMPETITOR] shows up for [SEARCH]" — worth 10 minutes?"

### Day 3 — Follow-up #1

Short, useful: link to the Punctual Plumbers case study (`#proof`). Not pushy.

### Day 7 — Follow-up #2

Last bump, low pressure. Offer the "map, not a deck" framing. If no reply → **move to nurture** (never dead-email).

### Nurture (if not ready)

6 touches over ~3 months via `docs/06-operations/templates/nurture-sequence.md`.

---

## The sale (Exposure Audit)

- Price: **$495** (≈ R8,900)
- Payment: **collected at booking** (Cal.com payment) — no invoicing friction
- Turnaround: **5 business days** from payment + intake
- Deliverable: visibility breakdown map + fixes list + walkthrough call

### What makes them say yes

1. **Low risk:** $495 << the tactics they've already wasted money on
2. **Shows work:** Punctual Plumbers case is proof, not promise
3. **Specific:** we've already found where they're invisible (the email observation)
4. **Time-bound:** 5 days, not a 10-page report they'll never read

## After the audit (feed the flywheel)

Each audit becomes:
- A **public case study** (with permission)
- A **Build / Compound** upsell if it's a fit
- A **referral ask**: "Know another business invisible online?"

## Rules

- **Qualify, don't pitch to everyone.** 20 great fit candidates > 50 random.
- **Start SA.** Prove locally, expand to UAE/UK/AU later.
- **$495 is a foot in the door, not a negotiation.** If they need Build/Compound, that's the follow-up.
- **Ask permission** before publishing any client's numbers.
